# Local Euler Factors and the Arithmetic-Chiral/Deninger Problem

The Gaussian Tate integral and the finite-prime Euler integrals assemble to
the completed Riemann zeta function in their common domain of absolute
convergence. The same finite Euler factor is also a local heat trace and gives
a normalized Toeplitz KMS state, but these scalar and operator identities do
not determine an augmented algebra or a bar construction.

The paper proves this obstruction concretely, states Deninger's determinant
formula and its Hilbert--Pólya consequence under their full hypotheses, gives
Weil's complete explicit-formula criterion, and records Kim's classical
finite-gauge arithmetic Chern--Simons invariant. The arithmetic-chiral,
continuous-quantum, Wilson-surface, and comparison constructions are posed as
typed open problems.
