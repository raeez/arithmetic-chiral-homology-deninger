# Local Euler Factors and the Arithmetic-Chiral/Deninger Problem

Raeez Lorgat

The paper compares local Tate integrals, prime equilibrium operators, and the integral bar construction of the divisor monoid. It constructs positive sectors of the full Weil form and curve-zeta Hall operator exchanges. Deninger’s determinant formula retains its spectral hypotheses, and the arithmetic-chiral comparison remains an open construction problem.
